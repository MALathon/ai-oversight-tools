AI Oversight Tools Build Plan

IRB-focused AI risk assessment toolkit

Tools

/risk-matrix
Risk Matrix
For IRB Reviewers
3x3 grid mapping Stage x Patient Impact to determine risk and oversight levels. Visual, interactive risk classification.
/reviewer
Reviewer Checklist
For IRB Reviewers
Structured review prompts organized by stage and model type, mapped to CFR criteria. Tracks completion percentage.
/protocol-builder
Protocol Builder
For PIs / Study Teams
Guided assessment flow: answer questions, identify risks, select mitigations, export protocol as .docx.
/admin
Admin / Traceability Editor
For Developers / Admins
Graph editor for managing entity linkages across the knowledge graph. View and edit traceability connections.

Three-Stage Framework

Stage 1: Discovery & Ideation
  • Early concept exploration
  • Initial feasibility assessment
  • Minimal patient exposure risk
Stage 2: Analytic & Performance Validation
  • Retrospective data analysis
  • Model training & evaluation
  • Moderate risk considerations
Stage 3: Real-World Deployment
  • Prospective clinical use
  • Patient-facing decisions
  • Full oversight required
